A.R.I.A Studio — Terms of Service

A.R.I.A Studio is a personal, non-commercial automation tool operated solely by its developer (the "Operator") for his own TikTok account. It is not distributed to, and cannot be used by, third parties.

What the app does

  • Authorizes the Operator's own TikTok account via Login Kit (OAuth 2.0 / PKCE).
  • Uploads short videos created with the Operator's own AI pipeline to his own account as drafts via the Content Posting API. Every upload is reviewed and published manually by the Operator.
  • Reads profile and video statistics of that same account via the Display API.

Acceptable use

The app complies with TikTok's Developer Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. Content is reviewed by a human before publishing and is labelled as AI-generated (AIGC) where required by TikTok policies.
