A.R.I.A Studio — Privacy Policy

This policy describes how A.R.I.A Studio, a personal tool operated by Jose Perez Martinez, handles data when connecting to TikTok via the TikTok for Developers APIs.

Data the app accesses

  • Open ID / union ID and basic profile information of the Operator's own TikTok account (user.info.basic).
  • The list of videos posted by that account and their public metrics (video.list).
  • No access to any other user's data. No private messages, no contacts, no behavioral data.

Where data goes

  • OAuth tokens are stored locally, encrypted, owner-only, on the Operator's own computer.
  • The tool communicates only with official TikTok API endpoints (open.tiktokapis.com and its upload hosts) plus TikTok's OAuth screens.
  • No data is sold, shared with, or sent to any third party. No analytics, no tracking, no advertising.

Retention

OAuth access tokens are short-lived (24 hours) and refreshed locally as needed. Refresh tokens persist locally until the Operator revokes them or uninstalls the tool. Users can revoke access at any time from TikTok's "Manage app permissions" settings.
